---
title: "Try Sandbox"
---

The **Try WhatsApp Sandbox on Soca Platform** feature is specifically designed as a secure environment for _testing_ your _AI Chat_ solutions before they go _live_ on an official WABA number.

- **Real-Time Simulation**: Experience how your AI Chatbot responds to customer interactions on WhatsApp, from simple to complex scenarios.
- **Testing Without Your Own WABA Number**: We provide a _test_ number (Sandbox) so you can start trying it out immediately without needing to register or configure a new WABA number. This is ideal for _developer_ and _product_ teams who need fast concept validation.
- **Exclusive Access**: This function ensures that the _AI Chat_ integration process runs smoothly and is minimally _buggy_ when implemented on your actual WABA number in the future.
