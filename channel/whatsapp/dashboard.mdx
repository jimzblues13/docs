### Usage Monitoring & In-Depth Customer Behavior Analysis

- **Critical Message Usage Monitoring:** Gain full visibility into key metrics such as **Total Messages Sent & Received**, **Average AI Response Time (Avg Response Time AI)**, and a breakdown of the **Number of Outbound** and **Inbound Messages**.
- **Identify Peak Activity & Conversation Traffic:** See when your customers are most actively interacting for strategic resource placement. Understand your customers' communication patterns through **Conversation Traffic** analysis on the Soci platform.
- **History-Based Customer Behavior Analysis:** **Innovation!** You can now **analyze customer patterns and sentiments** by directly querying your **AI Chatbot** about the content of the entire WhatsApp conversation history. Identify _pain points_, trending products, and complaint categories without manually reading every single _chat_.

### Why Should You Use This Dashboard?

- **Cost and Efficiency Optimization:** By monitoring _Total Messages_ and _Peak Activity_, you can allocate resources more smartly, reduce operational costs, and ensure no messages are missed during busy hours.
- **Elevate Service Quality (Customer Experience) & Sales Strategy:** Monitoring the _Avg Response Time AI_ guarantees fast responses. Furthermore, the **AI Conversation History Analysis** feature allows you to make _marketing_ and _product development_ decisions based on **specific customer behavioral insights** **pulled directly from their conversation data.**
