### Full Control in One Inbox

The Soca Inbox is not just a place to receive messages; it’s the command center for your Customer Service (CS) team:

- **Real-Time Conversation Monitoring:** Oversee all interactions occurring between the **End User** and the **AI Chatbot** directly. View the complete conversation history to understand the context of the customer's issue.
- **Human Takeover & Switch:** Deliver superior service with instant interruption capability. When a conversation becomes too complex or requires human empathy, the CS Agent can **immediately take over** from the AI Chatbot with a single _switch_ button click. The conversation will be seamlessly transitioned from the AI to the _Human Agent_ without requiring the _End User_ to repeat their message.
- **Task Management (Ticketing):** Allocate, prioritize, and track customer cases directly from the inbox to ensure no query is missed and all issues are resolved quickly.

### Why Your Business Must Use Soca Inbox?

Combine the speed of AI and the emotional intelligence of humans for maximum results:

- **Boost Efficiency and Customer Satisfaction (CSAT):**
  - The AI Chatbot handles **80%** of repetitive and simple questions quickly (**24/7**).
  - _Human Agents_ can focus on the **20%** of complex cases requiring in-depth resolution. The result? Faster response times, better quality answers, and **increased customer loyalty**.
- **Optimize Operational Costs:**
  - Reduce your Agents' workload and allocate human resources more wisely. Investing in this intelligent inbox will cut your CS operational costs in the long run.
- **Accurate Data & Insights:**
  - Every interaction is recorded. Analyze AI performance, _Human Agent_ speed, and customer query trends to continuously refine your service strategy.
