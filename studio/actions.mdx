Action Nodes allow you to integrate various specific functions and capabilities into interaction flows, both through text-based conversations (Chat/Whatsapp) and voice calls.

**Available Actions:**

**Conversation Chat & Whatsapp**

- **Q&A (Question & Answer):** Utilizes knowledge sources (Docs/Table/Audio/JSON) to answer user questions directly and informatively. AI will search for the most relevant answer based on the question provided.
- **Generate Chart:** Leverages data from knowledge sources (Table) to generate various types of data visualizations such as bar, line, or pie charts. This allows AI to present information visually to users.
- **Generate File:** Creates and sends various types of files to users based on information processed in the workflow. For example, generating document summaries in Docx format or creating reports in Excel format.
- **Vision OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** Enables AI to extract text from images uploaded by users. The extracted text can then be used to generate summaries or create reports.

**Conversation Voice Call**

- **Inbound:** Manages incoming call flows, including automatic answering and initial information gathering.
- **Outbound:** Automates outgoing calls for various purposes, such as reminders, verifications, or proactively conveying information to users.
- **Transfer Call:** Directs calls to human agents or appropriate departments based on user needs or information gathered during the AI interaction.
- **Notification**_\*:_ Sends notifications to users through various channels (WhatsApp/Email/In-App) with content generated based on prompt instructions. Notifications can be sent immediately or scheduled as needed.

  _\*can be used for Conversation Chat/Whatsapp_

**Why are Action Nodes Important?**

- **Extended Functionality:** Enables interaction flows to do more than just exchange messages or answer simple questions.
- **Richer Interactions:** Provides more engaging and informative ways to interact with users through data visualization (Chat/Whatsapp) and quick voice responses (Voice Call).
- **Task Automation:** Automates the creation and sending of files (Chat/Whatsapp), call management (Voice Call), and sending notifications.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Speeds up interaction handling, reduces waiting times (Voice Call), and optimizes resources.
- **Flexibility in Interaction:** Allows for intelligent call routing (Voice Call) and the integration of various capabilities within text conversations (Chat/Whatsapp).
